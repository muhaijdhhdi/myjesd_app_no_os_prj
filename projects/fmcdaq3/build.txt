从官方的no-os库克隆下来并编译构建的步骤
1.git clone https://github.com/muhaijdhhdi/myjesd_app_no_os_prj ~/vivado-project/high-speed/zu9/adi-example-prj/no-os
2.cd ~/vivado-project/high-speed/zu9/adi-example-prj/no-os/projects/fmcdaq3
3.将.xsa copy到这里，然后再进行将vitis环境source到这里， source /home/hwt/tool/xilinx-2025-unified/2025.1/Vitis/settings64.sh
4.make
5.编译失败,需要先make reset后再make,生成BOOT.BIN,fmcdaq3.elf
6.将程序烧写到FPGA 
source /home/hwt/tool/xilinx-2025-unified/2025.1/Vitis/settings64.sh
  cd /home/hwt/vivado-project/high-speed/zu9/adi-example-prj/no-os/projects/
  fmcdaq3
  make run -j1
  make run已经修改了，确保能够下载数据下去
